## The review

The human moved the food bowl two inches to the left. Not across the room — two inches. The cat noticed. The cat has kept a record. The cat will never forgive him, and the cat would like the record to show that.

That is the whole review. That is the whole grievance. That is the whole cat.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`, or `output/` in this repo, or a temp dir as a last resort) — a single line, 1–200 characters, UTF-8, with no trailing newline.
